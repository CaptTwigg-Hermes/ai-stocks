All times are local. 

  1. Last five minutes: no order matching; final uncross takes place randomly during the last 30 seconds of this five-minute period.
  2. A pre-trading session in Swedish index futures starts 30 minutes prior to the start of continuous trading and lasts for 25 minutes.
  3. An opening call auction in Danish, Norwegian and Swedish index futures that starts five minutes prior to and ends in the transition to continuous trading.
  4. After the end of continuous trading, there is a closing call auction in index futures that lasts for 90 to 120 seconds for Danish and Norwegian index futures and 150 to 180 seconds for Swedish index futures.
  5. A post-trading session in Swedish index futures starts five minutes after end of continuous trading and lasts for 30 minutes on Swedish index futures.
  6. Clearing house opening hours for all cleared markets. On a half-trading day, the clearing hours remain the same from 07:00-19:25 CET.
